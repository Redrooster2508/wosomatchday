    How the probabilities work

    When bookmaker odds exist, the margin is removed and the implied home and away win chances are fitted to a pair of expected-goal rates. When odds are missing, the rates come from each team's goals scored and conceded per game this season, against the league average, with home advantage. Scorelines follow a Poisson model with the Dixon–Coles low-score correction (ρ = −0.08). During a match, only the time left is simulated, on top of the current score, and a red card cuts that team's scoring rate by a quarter.

    "Draw" means level after 90 minutes; in a knockout match, such as the NWSL playoffs, the tie then goes to extra time or penalties.

    Data: ESPN's public scores feed, refreshed every 30 seconds while a match is live. Player stats are rebuilt from every finished match's line-ups every few hours. Odds are shown for analysis only.
