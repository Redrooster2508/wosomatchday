# Bracket

`bracket.js`.

## Knockout rounds from the feed
- Phases come from the scoreboard's `list` calendar (labels such as "Playoffs - Quarterfinals").
- Only phases that have **started** are requested: short ones day by day, long ones as a whole-year list filtered by date.
- Two-leg ties are grouped by team pair, with aggregate scores.
- If any request fails, the bracket says so instead of showing a projection.

## NWSL
Fixed bracket: 1 v 8 and 4 v 5 meet in one semi-final, 2 v 7 and 3 v 6 in the other. Higher seeds host; the final is neutral. **Title chances** are combined exactly through the bracket from each tie's [[Probability model]] chances.

On 2026-11-06: Gotham 25%, San Diego 23%, Washington 17%, Kansas City 15%.

## Champions League
Until the knockouts start on 18 Dec 2026, the bracket shows a projection from the league phase: top 4 go straight to the quarter-finals, 5th–12th play the knockout play-offs (5–8 seeded against 9–12, decided by the draw), and 13th–18th are eliminated.
