## 40. "one main page for each team (club or country)" and "improve appearance" (build 22)
- One page per team, whichever tab it's opened from: Chelsea's shows WSL and Champions League together. The all-competition feed names the competitions; each one the site covers is also asked directly, because the feed's results can come back empty (Barcelona).
- Main competition: the domestic league for clubs, otherwise the one in the address. It sets the masthead and record. Squads, heatmaps and head to head add every competition together; forecasts show one block each.
- Appearance: a colour per competition (`--k0`…`--k3`, light and dark) on labels and a bar down each row; short labels with the confederation dropped; ten results and ten fixtures; a narrower head to head table; men's teams get a crest on their colours instead of an empty photo space.
- (Corrected in 42: men's club ids don't collide. Arsenal is 359 in the Premier League and the Champions League, and the all feed for 359 gives men's Arsenal's matches as it should.)
